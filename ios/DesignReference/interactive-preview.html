Appearance

Semantic colors. Dark appearance preserves readable contrast.

Actual Dynamic Type and VoiceOver require iPhone validation.

Interaction contract

  • Primary actions: at least 56 pt.
  • Read heading, state, grouped details, then actions.
  • Mute alerts leaves command listening on.
  • Pause analysis preserves user control.
  • Stop listening ends the microphone session.
  • Old observations are never replayed.

Camera directions

Drone mode says camera-left and camera-right. No distance or personal direction is claimed without evidence.
